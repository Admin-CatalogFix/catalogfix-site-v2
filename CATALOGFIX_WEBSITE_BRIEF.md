## 1. Build setup

- Build in a **new GitHub Pages repo** (e.g. `catalogfix-site-v2`). Do not touch the current site repo.
- Preview on the github.io URL. Move the custom domain only when Carl approves.
- **3 pages:** Home · Who We Are · Our Team
- **Contact section at the bottom of every page.** No separate Contact page.
- Always a **light** design. A designer will refine visuals later. Content and positioning come first.
- Writing style: plain, simple English. Short sentences. No hype.

## 2. Brand basics

- Colours: Primary Orange `#E88130`, Medium Navy `#223F5A`, Dark Navy `#11283E`, Deep Orange `#D3671D`, Charcoal `#111111`, Soft White `#F6F6F6`, Pale Navy `#DDE3EA` (lines/borders only, never text).
- Type: Helvetica Neue for everything (Bold headings, Regular body). ITC Souvenir is for the logo only.
- No gradients, no drop shadows, no decorative patterns, no stock-photo smiles.
- Logo files: Canva brand kit "CatalogFix". Primary lockup in the top menu.
- Banned words: "stewardship", "compounding", "world-class", "game-changing", "cutting-edge".

## 3. Positioning rules

- CatalogFix is a **specialist network that works as part of the client's team**, not a vendor.
- "Not an agency" is only used as a contrast, never as the intro.
- **Don't centre on Slack.** Most clients use WhatsApp or email. Say "whatever channels you already use."
- **Never say "no account manager."** Carl oversees accounts.
- Strongest point vs in-house hiring: **no hiring cost, no ramp-up, no training.**
- **Claims to avoid:** "no restrictions", "dozens of catalogs", pattern knowledge across clients, backup/coverage if someone is out, "weekly" reports (say "regular" until the Friday report is live for every client).

## 4. Visual direction

- Avoid the "AI/vibe-coded" look: no centred-everything hero, no three identical icon cards, no emoji icons.
- **Split hero:** headline left, visual right.
- Hero visual: an annotated listing (real but blurred, or a clean mock), ideally before/after. Small **orange** notes on specific fixes ("Title rewritten", "Suppression cleared", "Main image restored"). No green checkmarks, no Amazon logo, no drop shadow. Source idea: Canva deck `DAHVLIqiTpg`, slide 1.
- Vary section layouts. Big bold headlines, quiet body text, lots of white space.
- The logo loop/arrow can be used large and cropped, or as a thin orange line.
- Comparison table: scrolls sideways or becomes stacked cards on mobile.

## 6. WHO WE ARE PAGE (page 2, 3 parts)

Small jump menu at top: Who we are · How we're different · What we do

### 6.1 Who we are
**Headline:** Direct expert support, as part of your team.

CatalogFix was created with one purpose: to give sellers direct expert support, with no agency layers in between. We're a team of Amazon and Walmart specialists who came together to work in a way that feels fair, honest, and actually helpful to the brands we work with.

Many of us came from agencies. We know how that system works: one person handling 10 to 15 clients, rushing the work, with only a few hours of real focus for each account. That setup doesn't let specialists do their best work, and clients feel it.

So we built something different. We work as part of your team. Fair to you, and fair to the people doing the work.

> OPEN: confirm "Many of us came from agencies" is true for most of the team. If not, use "Our founder came from agencies."

### 6.2 How we're different
**Headline:** What changes when you work with us.

1. **You talk to the person doing the work.** No layers, no messages passed along.
2. **You pay for the work.** No overhead fees, no inflated retainers.
3. **Honest calls.** If something won't work, we tell you once, clearly. Then we respect your decision.

### 6.3 What we do
**Headline:** Where we can help.

**Intro:** For Amazon and Walmart. Pick one service or several, and each one is handled by a specialist in that area.

1. **Catalog & SEO:** catalog errors, variations, suppressed listings, compliance fixes, listing copy, and localization
2. **Design & content:** product images, A+ content, Walmart rich media, storefronts
3. **Advertising:** Amazon PPC, Walmart Connect
4. **Reimbursements:** lost and damaged inventory claims
5. **Full account management:** everything above, handled together

**Marketplace line (its own line):** We cover every Amazon marketplace, including the US, CA, UK, EU, AU, and JP.

## 8. Free audit Google Form
Focused on **one issue**, not a full audit. Under 2 minutes.

1. Name
2. Email
3. Brand name
4. Where do you sell? (checkboxes: Amazon US, CA, UK, EU, AU, JP, Walmart)
5. What do you want us to look at? (dropdown: Sales are dropping / Listings suppressed or showing errors / Ads cost too much / Not ranking for key searches / Account health issues / Launching a new product / Other)
6. Which product? (link or ASIN)
7. Tell us a bit more about the issue
8. Who handles Amazon for you now? (optional: Myself / In-house person / Agency / Freelancer)

Do not ask for: competitors, Seller Central access, sales figures.

**Confirmation message:** Thanks. Our team will look into the issue you described and send your audit to this email.
