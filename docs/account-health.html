What we handle

The issues that put your account or listings at risk.

  • Account health: looking into policy warnings and performance issues, and fixing what's behind them.
  • Listing violations: fixing listings flagged for restricted words, claims or missing information.
  • Seller Support cases: opening cases with Amazon and following them up until they're resolved.
  • Suspension appeals: writing appeals when a listing or your account is suspended. We'll tell you honestly what your chances are. Amazon makes the final call.

How we work

Fix the cause, not just the warning.

  • Review: we read the warning or suspension notice and check your account history.
  • Find the cause: we work out what triggered it, such as a listing, a process or a complaint.
  • Fix and respond: we fix the cause, then write a clear response or appeal.
  • Follow up: we keep the case moving until Amazon replies, and tell you where it stands.
