Case study · Wildfang

From €29K to €64K a month on Amazon US.

Wildfang came to us with a catalog that had grown messy across a dozen Amazon marketplaces, and nobody clearly in charge of it. In 14 months we rebuilt it from the ground up, took it into new countries, and more than doubled monthly sales on Amazon US.

The brand

A German pet brand selling natural dog chews, toys and food.

Wildfang makes natural dog chews (coffee wood, olive wood, briar root, deer antler, cattle horn and hard cheese), rubber toys, dry and wet dog food, Vetline supplements, salmon oil and puppy bundles.

It sells on Amazon in the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy and Spain, and since working with us also in the Netherlands, Belgium, Poland and Sweden. It also sells on Walmart and eBay in the US.

Where they started

Lots of products, lots of countries, and nobody owning the whole picture.

When we took over the account in July 2025, Jens Vogel, Wildfang’s Managing Director, described a catalog that “had grown messy over time”:

  • Inconsistent listings and broken variations, so sizes and versions of the same product didn’t sit together.
  • Suppressed ASINs that couldn’t sell, including a listing that had to be reinstated in Italy.
  • Weak SEO, with listings that weren’t written for each country’s shoppers.
  • PPC that wasn’t scaling efficiently.
  • No clear ownership or long-term plan.

What we did

Five steps, in order of what was costing the most sales.

  1. Fixed the foundation

    We rebuilt the parent–child variations for all listings in the US, Canada, the UK, Germany, France, Italy and Spain, and got the Italian listing reinstated. We fixed product types, main images, a restricted ASIN in Germany, listings showing “out of stock” despite FBA stock, and stranded inventory in the UK. We also removed the word “indestructible” from every marketplace, a claim Amazon can flag.

  2. Spoke each shopper’s language

    Instead of copying the English listings, we did keyword research and wrote new listing copy in German, French, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Polish and Swedish, for every product line from antler chews to puppy bundles. We translated the text on product images and the brand story too.

  3. Opened new marketplaces

    We launched the range in the Netherlands, Belgium, Poland and Sweden with localized listings, and set up a separate ad portfolio for the new markets.

  4. Made the listings sell

    We built Premium A+ Content for seven product lines in the US and Canada (Coffee Wood, Coffee Wood with Salmon Oil, Olive Wood, Briar Root, Leather Bone, Rubber Toys and Hard Cheese), plus new listing images. We ran Subscribe & Save, Brand Tailored Promotions and Prime Exclusive Discounts.

  5. Scaled the ads, and kept watching

    We set up hero campaigns for every single-pack product in Germany, ran discount campaigns, and reviewed performance across all markets every month. Every week we check account health in every marketplace and send Wildfang a progress report.

We did the same on Walmart and eBay: listing setup and SEO, variation fixes, Walmart ads and WFS inventory, and the eBay US launch.

The result

Monthly sales on Amazon US more than doubled in 14 months.
