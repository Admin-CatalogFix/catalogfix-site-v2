What we fix and build

The work behind every listing that sells.

  • Listing creation: setting up new products correctly from the start, with the right category, attributes and identifiers.
  • Variations: setting up, fixing and restructuring parent–child variations, including ones that won't attach or have split apart.
  • Error fixes: finding and fixing listings that show errors, wrong details or failed uploads.
  • Suppressed listings and compliance: finding out why a listing was suppressed or blocked, like a supplement flagged for medical claims, then fixing it and getting it reinstated.
  • Amazon SEO: keyword research and rewritten titles, bullet points, descriptions and backend search terms, so your products show up for the searches your customers use.
  • Localization: adapting listings for each marketplace instead of copying the US listing.
  • Walmart: item setup, listing optimization, and fixing item setup and compliance errors.

How we work

Problems that block sales get fixed first.

  • Audit: we check your catalog for errors, suppressions, missing details and weak keywords.
  • Fix in order of impact: listings that can't sell come first, then the ones that sell below what they could.
  • Optimize: keyword research, then listing copy written for shoppers and for Amazon search.
  • Check: we confirm each listing is live, shows the right details, and is indexed for its main keywords.
  • Keep watching: Amazon sometimes changes listings on its own, so we check they stay correct.
