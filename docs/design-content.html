Design & content

Images and content that help shoppers decide.

Shoppers can't pick your product up, so your images and content have to answer their questions. We design them to Amazon's rules and for the phone screen most shoppers use.

See all services

What we design

Everything a shopper sees on your product page.

  • Main image: a clean product photo on a pure white background that meets Amazon's image rules, so it isn't flagged or suppressed.
  • Secondary images: infographics, size and dimension images, lifestyle images and comparisons that answer shoppers' questions.
  • A+ Content: the extra images and text further down your product page, including comparison charts across your range.
  • Brand Store: design and setup of your Amazon Brand Store, so shoppers can browse your whole range.
  • Walmart rich media: enhanced images and content for your Walmart listings.

How we work

You approve the plan before we design.

  • Brief: we look at your reviews, questions and competitors to find what shoppers need to know.
  • Layout: we show you a rough layout of each image or module for your OK.
  • Design: final images, readable on a phone screen, with text kept short.
  • Upload and check: we upload them and check they show correctly on desktop and mobile.
