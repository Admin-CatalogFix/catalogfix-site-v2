About CatalogFix

What we stand for.

Our mission

To give sellers direct expert support, with no agency layers in between.

Our vision

Marketplace support where the person you talk to is the person doing the work, and that person is paid fairly for doing it well.

Our core values

  1. Ownership.

    We treat your account like it's ours. If something breaks, we own it until it's fixed, not just reported.

  2. No layers.

    You talk to the person doing the work. Nothing gets lost being passed along.

  3. Get to the root.

    A quick fix isn't a fix if the problem comes back. We keep digging until we know why something broke.

  4. Straight talk.

    If something won't work, we tell you once, clearly. Then we respect your decision.

  5. Fair to both sides.

    Fair to you, and fair to the people doing the work. We pay our team well because the work is worth it.
