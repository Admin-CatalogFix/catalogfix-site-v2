About CatalogFix

What we stand for.

Our mission

Our aim is to give clients direct expert support from people who actually do the work — with ownership, guidance, and attention to every detail.

Our vision

Our vision is to build and protect a team of seasoned Amazon specialists united by ownership, fairness, and respect for talent — working in a culture that empowers and supports real expertise.

Our core values

  1. 1. Transparency First

    We don't follow rigid agency playbooks. We operate as experts, making informed decisions based on your catalog, data, and goals — and we're transparent about every call we make.

    Trust begins with honesty, and we commit to that from day one.

  2. 2. Expert-Led Execution

    Your catalog is handled directly by seasoned Amazon specialists with real, hands-on experience managing live accounts. No juniors guessing, no outsourcing, no shortcuts.

    You're paying for experts — and that's exactly who touches your catalog.

  3. 3. Ownership & Accountability

    We treat your catalog like it's our own. If something breaks, we fix it. If something needs improvement, we act on it. We don't wait for issues to escalate — we stay proactive.

    Your success becomes our responsibility.

  4. 4. Respect for Time & Talent

    Good work requires focus, and we value that. We don't overload our team or stretch them thin — they have the time and support to deliver high-quality results for your brand.

    Well-supported people deliver better results — and your brand benefits from that.

  5. 5. Partnership, Not Transaction

    We don't operate on a task-only mindset. We work with you as an extension of your team — guiding decisions, sharing context, and helping you scale your catalog with confidence.

    We grow alongside you through long-term partnership, not one-off work.

  6. 6. Straightforward Communication

    No BS. No runaround. No agency-style layers. We communicate clearly and directly, making collaboration easy and decisions faster.

    Good communication saves time — and keeps everything moving smoothly.

  7. 7. Long-Term Mindset

    We build and maintain catalogs designed to scale over time. Whether you're expanding across the US, CA, UK, or EU, we prioritize sustainable performance over short-term fixes.

    A healthy catalog leads to a healthier, long-lasting business.
