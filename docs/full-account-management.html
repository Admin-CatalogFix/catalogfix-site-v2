What's included

Every service, working as one plan.

  • One team covering your catalog, SEO, ads, creatives, reimbursements and account health.
  • Carl oversees your account, and each area is handled by its specialist.

Your reports

Sent by email to every client, and included in your fee.

  • Every week, progress: what we finished and anything that's blocked. Every two weeks if you prefer.
  • Every week, month to date: sales in total and per product, and advertising performance in total and for your top campaigns.
  • Every week, account health: your account health status, Buy Box, Best Sellers Rank (BSR) and keyword rankings.
  • Every month, last month's performance: how the previous month went.
  • Every month, profitability: profit and loss, with your income and expenses.
