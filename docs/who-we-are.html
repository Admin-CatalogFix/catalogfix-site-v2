CatalogFix was created with one purpose: to give sellers direct expert support, with no agency layers in between. We're a team of Amazon and Walmart specialists who came together to work in a way that feels fair, honest, and actually helpful to the brands we work with.

Our founder came from agencies. We know how that system works: one person handling 10 to 15 clients, rushing the work, with only a few hours of real focus for each account. That setup doesn't let specialists do their best work, and clients feel it.

So we built something different. We work as part of your team. Fair to you, and fair to the people doing the work.
