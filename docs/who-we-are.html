Who we are

Direct expert support,
as part of your team.

Who we are How we're different What we do

CatalogFix was created with one purpose: to give sellers direct expert support, with no agency layers in between.

We're a team of Amazon and Walmart specialists who came together to work in a way that feels fair, honest, and actually helpful to the brands we work with.

Many of us came from agencies. We know how that system works: one person handling 10 to 15 clients, rushing the work, with only a few hours of real focus for each account. That setup doesn't let specialists do their best work, and clients feel it.

So we built something different. We work as part of your team. Fair to you, and fair to the people doing the work.

How we're different

What changes when you work with us.

  1. 01

    You talk to the person doing the work.

    Your specialist talks to you directly, on WhatsApp, email or whatever you already use. Nothing gets lost being passed along.

    What this means for youAsk why a listing changed or a bid moved, and the answer comes from the person who made the change, not a summary of someone else's work.

  2. 02

    We find problems before they cost you.

    Amazon doesn't email you when a listing goes down. We check your account ourselves instead of waiting for you to notice.

    What this means for youSuppressed listings, listing errors and policy warnings: we look for them regularly, so they're fixed before they cost you weeks of sales.

  3. 03

    We fix the cause, not the symptom.

    When something keeps breaking, we keep digging until we know why, then stop it at the source.

    What this means for youIf the same problem keeps coming back, a quick fix isn't a fix. We work it through with Amazon until we find what's behind it, so it stays fixed.
