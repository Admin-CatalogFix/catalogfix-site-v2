Reimbursements

Getting back money Amazon owes you.

When Amazon loses or damages your FBA inventory, you're often owed money, and it isn't always paid automatically. We find it, file the claims and follow them up.

See all services

What we claim for

Inventory that went missing or was damaged in Amazon's hands.

  • Lost in the warehouse: units that disappeared from Amazon's fulfillment centers.
  • Damaged in the warehouse: units damaged while Amazon was storing or handling them.
  • Lost shipments: units that went missing on the way in, when Amazon received fewer than you sent.
  • Tracking: a record of every claim, its status and what's been paid.

How we work

Amazon only accepts claims for a limited time, so we check regularly.

  • Check your reports: we compare your inventory and shipment reports to find units you haven't been paid for.
  • File with evidence: each claim goes in with the details Amazon asks for.
  • Follow up: we stay on every claim until it's paid or closed.
  • Report: you see what was claimed and what was recovered.
